# 5. Impact

## 5.1 Local — Abu Dhabi / UAE

**Prioritised action.** The 105 ranked hotspots answer a specific question for
regulators: given limited enforcement, monitoring, or restoration budget, where
first? Rather than a generic habitat map, they rank concrete locations by habitat
value × real pressure — directly informing where a protected-area boundary
review, a dredging-permit condition, or a monitoring buoy should go.

**A monitoring baseline, not a snapshot.** Built entirely on free, continuously
updated Sentinel-2 / Landsat data, the pipeline re-runs at near-zero marginal
cost; the 2019→2024 change layer already shows it can track whether hotspots
stabilise, worsen, or respond to intervention.

**Serving the reserve's mandate.** The Marawah Reserve's UNESCO / MPA status
commits it to evidence-based management; the 3× zonal finding gives that mandate
a concrete, quantified starting point rather than a general claim that "the core
matters."

## 5.2 Regional and global

The exposure × consequence structure, threat factors, and validation approach
are not specific to seagrass or the Gulf: the same recipe — combine a
habitat-value layer with a weighted, distance-decayed threat overlay, validate
against ground truth, stress-test the weights — transfers to other
single-species, satellite-visible marine habitats, from dugongs elsewhere in the
Indo-Pacific to manatee seagrass in the Americas. Because it relies only on free
imagery and open tooling, the barrier to adoption is know-how, not cost or
licensing — relevant for lower-resource conservation programmes where commercial
imagery or bespoke GIS consulting is not realistic.

## 5.3 Scope of these claims

Dugong Watch is a working, validated prototype. The impact above is what its
outputs enable; realising it means putting the tool in front of the agencies who
would use it and replacing schematic inputs with official GIS layers where
available. Section 6 sets out these limitations in full — deliberately, because a
tool that documents its own assumptions is more trustworthy to act on, not less.
